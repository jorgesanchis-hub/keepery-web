Privacy policy

Keepery doesn’t collect your data. We have no way of seeing your passwords.

Effective from 6 November 2026.

Who is responsible

Jorge Sanchis, the seller of Keepery on the App Store. Email: keepery_support@icloud.com.

The app

What you keep in Keepery stays on your device, encrypted with your master password, which isn’t stored anywhere. Keepery has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It includes no third-party kits for any of those and doesn’t connect to any server of ours. This version doesn’t upload your vault to any cloud.

The permissions it asks for

  • Camera (iPhone and iPad): to scan the QR code when you add a one-time code. The image isn’t saved and doesn’t leave the device.
  • Face ID: to open your vault without typing your password, if you turn it on. The system handles your biometric data; Keepery doesn’t see it.

What goes through your system

Some features use services from Apple or your device. They send nothing to us:

  • AutoFill. So the keyboard can suggest your entries, Keepery registers their title, username and website with the system, never the password or code. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Backups. Your vault is included in your system backup to iCloud or your computer, if you use one. It stays encrypted with your master password.
  • Clipboard. What you copy stays on the clipboard for up to 60 seconds, unless you copy something else first.
  • Diagnostic reports. If you’ve agreed to share them in your device settings, Apple may make crash reports available to the developer. Apple handles them under its own policy.
  • Websites of your entries. If you open an entry’s website, it opens in your browser, which has its own policy.
  • Exports. If you export, you decide where the file goes. The spreadsheet isn’t encrypted: the app warns you about this.

This website

This website uses no cookies, trackers or third-party resources. The server that hosts it, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), may keep technical logs of each visit, such as the IP address, for as long as its own privacy policy sets out. They’re used to serve and protect the website, on the basis of legitimate interest.

If you write to support

We’ll use your address and what you tell us only to reply to you, on the basis of your request, and keep it for one year. Your email passes through iCloud Mail (Apple). Never send us your master password or any other password.

Your rights

You can request access to, or the correction, erasure, restriction or portability of, any data we hold, or object to its use, at keepery_support@icloud.com. You can also complain to your country’s data protection authority. You control what’s in the app: you can export or delete it whenever you like.

Changes

If Keepery changes what it does with data, for example with features that send it off the device, we’ll update this page first and say so in the app’s release notes.

Keepery has no cookies, trackers or analytics, on this website either.
